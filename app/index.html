Client holding statement

Meridian AI is investigating an unauthorised outbound email generated by Aria at approximately [time]. We suspended the agent, revoked its active credentials, and are preserving records. Current understanding: [verified scope]. We will provide an updated facts pack by [time] and support Northwind’s assessment and response.

Individual notice outline

Northwind Retail is contacting you about a security incident involving information associated with your account. An AI-supported service sent a file containing [data categories] to an unintended external address. We stopped the service and are investigating. The information could be used for unwanted contact or targeted scams. Be cautious with unexpected messages; never share passwords or verification codes. Contact [support channel] for help. We will provide updates if material facts change.
